"Building agents that take financial conversations end to end, not just the

**Voice AI Platform**, multi-agent workflows in more than ten languages. We built
our own voice stack rather than buying one, so the work runs from provider
selection across LLM, speech to text and text to speech, all the way up into the
application layer. Deep multi-agent workflows that take a problem end to end

### CASHe. Intern, then Product Analyst, then Associate PM. July 2023 to March 2026
"Two and a half years, three roles, three products: from building the data layer
to founding PM on two launches."

**KaratClub**, a mutual fund privilege platform. Founding PM. India's first
portfolio-powered privilege programme: a five lakh or larger mutual fund

a single unit. Wireframes through to a production app. Shown as "1st of its kind
in India".

**Karat Wealth**, fixed deposits and mutual funds. Founding PM. The platform
repositioned onto regulated fixed income: FDs, RDs and bonds sourced from partner
banks through Upswing. Led a team of fourteen from wireframes to launch across
the app, the web journey and the website, then built the analytics suite that ran
daily ops and the lifecycle campaigns that fed it. Shown as "+74% user to
investor conversion".

**13Karat**, peer to peer lending. Retail investors directly opposite borrowers.
On it from the day it went live through to a hundred crore. Built the complete
analytics suite in Python, SQL and Power BI across onboarding, investing and
withdrawals, shipped repayment tracking that cut customer queries by seventy
percent, and replaced brittle name matching with fuzzy logic to cut bank
verification failures by forty percent. Shown as "a hundred crore, scaled to,
from launch".

### Teenvesting. Founder. June 2020 to September 2022
"The first zero to one. Solo, during college." Financial literacy for teenagers

## About, oldest first, because it is a narrative

**01 Early Days.** Born and brought up in Hyderabad. Vidyaranya, a school with no
exams until eighth grade and no uniform at all. What it gave instead was room:
whole afternoons under the tamarind tree playing football, basketball, badminton,
whatever was going, and the freedom to be interested in something because it was
interesting rather than because it was marked. Physics was always closest: the
only subject that felt like taking something apart to find out why it worked.

**02 College.** BITS Pilani, Hyderabad, 2019 to 2024. Electrical engineering, a
master's in physics and a minor in finance, a combination picked precisely
because he could not choose between them. Newton, Schrodinger and Feynman on one
side, Buffett, Munger, Peter Lynch and Howard Marks on the other, both doing the
same thing: clear thinking under uncertainty. Feynman also supplied the reason to
start teaching, that you do not really understand something until you can explain
it simply. Hence Teenvesting.

**03 The Builder.** At CASHe, intern to founding PM, twice over. A peer to peer
lending platform, then Karat Wealth and KaratClub from first wireframes to
production apps, reaching more than fifty thousand investors. Plus the analytics
infrastructure the whole team ran on. Shown as "2 products as Founding PM".

**04 The AI.** Voice AI now: orchestrating LLM, STT and TTS providers into
natural conversations. "The mission hasn't changed. The medium is now your voice."

The section opens with: "I've spent my entire career making investing accessible.
First by educating, then by building, now by giving it a voice."
