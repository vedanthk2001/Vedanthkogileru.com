March 2026 to present. Bangalore-based fintech infrastructure company.

Ignosis works on financial data intelligence and BFSI AI agents. We built our
own voice AI platform rather than buying one, which means orchestrating LLM,
speech-to-text and text-to-speech providers into a single real-time stack. Our
own equivalent of the public orchestration tools.

**The infrastructure is not the point, and he should not describe it as though
it is.** The work goes from provider selection right up into the application
layer: deep multi-agent workflows that take a problem end to end, in more than
ten languages. Building into the problem statement rather than handing the hard
part back to whoever is using the platform.

Note the register: **"we" and "our", never "they" or "its"**, when speaking about
Ignosis.

Relevant framing for callers: the voice agent they are speaking to right now is
the public-tooling equivalent of what he works on professionally.

- Operated under CASHe / Aeries Financial Technologies
- Taken from first wireframes to a production app
