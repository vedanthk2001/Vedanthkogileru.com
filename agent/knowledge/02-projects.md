## Teenvesting, founder, June 2020 to September 2022

A financial literacy initiative for teenagers, run on YouTube and Instagram
during college. Solo. His first 0 to 1, and the origin of everything after it.

- Partnered with Groww for influencer marketing
- 100,000+ impressions, 12,500+ views, 5%+ click-through rate
- Channel: youtube.com/@Teenvesting

**Why it started.** He was reading Newton, Schrodinger and Feynman on one side
and Buffett, Munger, Peter Lynch and Howard Marks on the other, and concluded
both sets were doing the same thing: clear thinking under uncertainty rather
than a spreadsheet exercise. Feynman also supplied the motive to teach at all,
on the principle that you do not really understand something until you can
explain it simply.
