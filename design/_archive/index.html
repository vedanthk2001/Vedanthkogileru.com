Five directions

Three built on V alone, two on VK. Two directions were built, tested and cut: Light Cone (at 45° it is a chevron, not a letter) and Entropy Gradient (a dotted arm always loses to a solid one — it renders as a checkmark). All geometry sits on a 24‑unit grid, so 1.5 units = 1px at favicon size and no stroke falls below 1.5px at 16px. Every mark is one flat colour before it earns a second, and no direction uses more than three shapes after reduction.

The rows marked ⚠ are the hero artwork crushed to 16px untuned — that is the honest failure test. The grey block under each shows the art actually tuned for that size.

01Gravity WellV ONLY

Not straight lines — a real well. The arms come in shallow at the rim (62° off vertical) and steepen continuously to 26° at the bottom, which is the profile of an actual 1/r potential. The ball is the mass sitting at the bottom of the well it makes: the rubber‑sheet picture every physicist has drawn, reduced to a letter.

COLOUR

FAILS BY
A ball resting inside the curve was the obvious version and it does not work: solved numerically, a circle tangent to both walls cannot sit lower than y ≈ 12 on a 24 grid — it floats at mid‑height and reads as a bullet, not a particle. Hence the ball as the base. The remaining risk is a wine glass or a plumb bob.
02Unit VectorV ONLY

V with a caret over it is v̂, a unit vector — and the caret that makes it a unit vector is itself a V, at exactly the same 27°. It is set at 30% of the V, a little over half its stroke weight, and clears the cap line by 20% of the V’s height, so it reads as a modifier rather than a second letter.

FAILS BY

03Ground StateV ONLY

Horizontal lines drawn inside a well are its quantised energy levels — the states a particle is allowed to occupy. They shorten and thicken as they descend, and the bottom one is the ground state: the lowest energy the system can hold once everything absorbable has been absorbed.

One colour. Second colour on the levels, not the V — the well is the container, the levels are the state.
FAILS BY
Weakest wit of the three: a physicist reads energy eigenstates instantly, everyone else reads decoration, and there is no second layer for them to find. Two stacked horizontals inside a shape is also the signal‑strength glyph, and one crossbar low in a V edges toward an upside‑down A.
04Rotated VertexV + K

A K is a V rotated 90° against a vertical, so the monogram is one path definition used twice — and the junction is a scattering vertex: one worldline in, two out.

The V is deleted. What survives is the K — which by construction still contains the V, rotated. Stroke 2.8→3.0, junction dot r 1.8→2.1. The cost is real and unavoidable: your browser tab shows a K.
READS AS
a K with a heavy junction
COLOUR
One colour. Second colour on the rotated instance only — colouring the exact strokes that are “the V, again” is what makes a stranger notice the trick. Best colour logic of the five.
FAILS BY
A two‑letter VK in blue‑violet is VKontakte, and indigo‑600 makes that collision worse. The rotation also forces one compromise: matching the V and K arm length exactly is what makes the K unusually wide.
