Hero animation: current 2D vs two 3D treatments

Same particle count (600), same timing as the live site: 2200 ms chaos, 1800 ms collapse, name lands at ~4.0 s.

ACurrent, flat 2D

What ships today. Particles scatter on a plane and ease into the sampled letterforms.

B3D depth, static camera

Each particle gains a z coordinate and is perspective-projected: near ones are larger and darker, far ones smaller and fainter. They collapse toward z = 0, so the letterforms resolve out of a cloud that had real volume. No library, no rotation.

C3D depth + camera rotation

Same projection, but the whole cloud rotates around the vertical axis during chaos and decelerates to square-on as it settles. This is the one that unmistakably reads as 3D, because parallax is what sells depth, not shading.
